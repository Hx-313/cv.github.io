# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Software engineers, technical specialists, and career professionals who need an efficient, high-craft CV builder. They edit across multiple devices (work laptop, home PC), need fast formatting without friction, and want recruiter-friendly resumes with clickable project links and exact page budgeting.

## Product Purpose

A desktop-grade, browser-native CV editor and ATS optimizer that eliminates resume formatting pain. It provides:
- Live, pixel-exact A4 pagination that intelligently balances content across exact page targets (e.g. 2-page fit without awkward section orphan gaps).
- Inline hyperlink and rich formatting capabilities inside descriptions for portfolio and project proofs.
- Ergonomic keyboard-driven editing (`Ctrl+B`, `Ctrl+K`) and responsive workspace panes.
- Seamless cross-browser and cross-device persistence so work is never lost or desynced across machines.

## Brand Personality

- **3-Word Personality**: Surgical, Effortless, Developer-Native
- **Tone**: Focused, tactile, high-density utility without bloat
- **Emotional Goals**: Confident control over document output; zero anxiety about pagination breaks, lost edits, or ATS rejections

## Anti-references

- **Canva / Visual Builder Bloat**: No clunky floating drag handles, snap grids, or distracting non-standard decorations.
- **Generic SaaS Template Cliché**: No pastel/cream AI defaults, no decorative gradient buttons, no slow bloated animations.
- **Brittle Word Processor Formatting**: No hidden margins or random paragraph jumps where pressing enter throws the entire layout onto page 3.

## Design Principles

1. **What You See Is What Prints (WYSIWYG Fidelity)**: The on-screen A4 canvas is the literal print sheet. Spacing, typography, and page breaks match the exported PDF 1:1.
2. **Keyboard-First Ergonomics**: Power users shouldn't have to reach for a mouse to bold a term or add a link. Common formatting tasks have instant hotkeys.
3. **No Stranded Content (Intelligent Flow)**: Break content at granular boundaries (bullet-level or balanced spacing) rather than dumping whole sections across pages and leaving dead whitespace.
4. **Resilient Portability**: Edits must sync effortlessly across devices without heavy login walls or fragile single-browser storage.
5. **Clean ATS Compatibility**: Output semantic, machine-readable text and clean link anchors that ATS parsers and human recruiters can parse without corruption.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliant contrast on all UI chrome elements and text fields.
- Full keyboard navigability across form sections and modal overlays.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all UI transitions.
- High-contrast, clean typography suitable for screen readers and high-resolution print.
